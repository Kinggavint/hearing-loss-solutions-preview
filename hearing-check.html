Online Hearing Check

How Is Your Hearing, Really?

Eight questions, about two minutes. These are the same questions Dr. Cohen asks at the start of a first appointment, and your answers will tell you whether a full evaluation is worth booking.

This is a self-assessment, not a hearing test. It cannot measure your hearing or diagnose anything. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing is submitted, stored, or emailed. If you would rather skip straight to a real measurement, book a comprehensive evaluation.

0 of 8 answered
Question 1 of 8
Do you find yourself asking people to repeat themselves?
Question 2 of 8
In a restaurant or a room with background noise, is it hard to follow the conversation?
Question 3 of 8
Has anyone told you the television or radio is too loud?
Question 4 of 8
Is it harder to hear higher-pitched voices, such as children or women, or to hear on the phone?
Question 5 of 8
Do you hear ringing, buzzing, hissing, or other sound that has no source outside your head?
Question 6 of 8
Are you regularly around loud sound: live music, machinery, power tools, or firearms?
Question 7 of 8
Do you have dizziness, unsteadiness, or a spinning sensation?
Question 8 of 8
Have you noticed a sudden drop in hearing in one or both ears?
What Happens Next

What a Real Evaluation Adds.

A screening tells you whether to look further. A comprehensive evaluation tells you what is actually happening and what to do about it.

1

A Conversation First

We start with your history, your work, the rooms you struggle in, and the moments that pushed you to call. That context shapes everything that follows.

2

Full Diagnostic Testing

Air and bone conduction, speech testing in quiet and in noise, middle ear measures, and tinnitus assessment when indicated. Not a quick screening.

3

Results Explained in Plain Language

You see your audiogram, understand what it means, and leave with a written summary and a plan that includes every option, including doing nothing.

Sudden hearing change in the last 72 hours?

Do not wait for a screening result. Sudden sensorineural hearing loss is time-sensitive and treated best within days. We hold same-day slots for these cases.
