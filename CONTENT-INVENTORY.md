# Content Inventory — Old Site vs. New Site

Prepared 2026-08-10, after the call with Dr. Cohen.

She asked to "sit down and compare the two." This is that comparison, generated
from her live sitemap (`hearinglosssolutions.com/page-sitemap.xml`) against the
28 pages in the rebuild. Nothing here is a guess about what the old site had.

---

## The headline number

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Page URLs on the live site | **125** |
| …that are the same topic repeated for another city | **38** |
| …that are form "thank you" confirmations | **8** |
| **Distinct real topics** | **87** |
| Pages in the rebuild | **28** |
| Blog posts on the live site | 6 |
| Event pages on the live site | 7 |

**The 125 is not 125 subjects.** The old site was built as a service × city
grid — the same topic written four times for Sherman Oaks, Beverly Hills,
Encino, and Studio City:

| Topic | Live pages |
|---|---|
| Auditory brain therapy | 5 |
| Tinnitus treatment | 5 |
| Assistive listening devices | 4 |
| Bone-anchored devices | 4 |
| Cochlear implants | 4 |
| Custom hearing protection | 4 |
| Ear wax removal | 4 |
| Hearing aids | 4 |
| Hearing care | 4 |
| Hearing injuries | 4 |
| Hearing & balance disorders | 4 |
| Hearing care and conservation | 4 |
| Hearing loss / dementia / diabetes | 4 |
| Mobile clinic | 4 |

That pattern worked in 2018. Today search engines read four near-identical
pages as one page copied three times, and they tend to rank *none* of them
well. That is the single biggest reason the page count dropped — not that the
subject was removed.

The rebuild keeps the four city pages (`beverly-hills`, `encino`,
`studio-city`, plus Sherman Oaks as the main location) and points them at one
strong page per topic.

## What is genuinely gone — please review this list

These are real gaps, not consolidation. **Nothing here was dropped for SEO
reasons; they simply have no equivalent yet.** Four of them I would put back.

| Missing | Why it matters | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Privacy policy** | Standard legal page; also required by Google Ads and most form/analytics tools | **Restore.** Low effort. |
| **Website accessibility statement** | Legal exposure, and a hearing-and-vision practice is exactly who gets asked for one | **Restore.** Low effort. |
| **"Request Dr. Cohen to speak"** | She speaks constantly; this is a referral and authority channel with no replacement | **Restore.** Pairs with the new seminars section. |
| Blog (+ 6 posts) | Currently no blog on the new site | Decide: rebuild, or migrate only the posts that earn traffic |
| Event pages (7) | The new site has a seminars section but no per-event pages | Fill once she sends the event calendar |
| Visual office tour | Depends on photography we do not have | Revisit after the photo shoot |

Two things the old site had that the rebuild **already replaced**, so they are
not gaps:

| Old page | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| Online hearing loss quiz | `hearing-check.html` — eight questions, runs in the browser, nothing submitted |
| Patient reviews | `reviews.html` + the rotating reviews on the home page |
