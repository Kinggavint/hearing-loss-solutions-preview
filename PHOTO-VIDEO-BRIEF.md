## Video

The video section on the home page is built and live. It currently shows a
"coming soon" panel — designed, not a broken frame, but it should be real.

**Everything below can be shot on a phone.** Horizontal, phone braced or on a
tripod, a window or lamp in front of the subject (never behind), and a quiet
room. Quality of sound matters more than quality of picture.

| Video | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Welcome from Dr. Cohen** | 60–90 sec | Who you are, what makes the practice different, what a first visit is like. Talk to the camera like a patient in the room. This is the priority. |
| **Patient testimonials** | 30–60 sec each, 3–5 of them | One question: "What was life like before, and what is it like now?" Let them ramble; we cut it. |
| **What is tinnitus?** | 60–90 sec | Plain language. This is the one most likely to get found in search and cited by AI answers. |
| **Office tour** | 60 sec | Walk from the front door to the exam room, narrating. Replaces the old "visual office tour" page. |

**Where they go:** we upload to YouTube on your channel, then embed. That is
free, keeps the site fast, and builds a YouTube presence that helps you get
found — including by AI search tools, which cite video heavily.
