Mobile Hearing Clinic — let us come to you

Everyone deserves expert hearing care, whether or not they can make it to our office. Our Mobile Hearing Clinic brings the full experience — evaluation, fitting, and follow-up — home to you.

Bringing Hearing Home

Concierge hearing care at your door.

Many patients cannot easily drive to appointments or arrange transportation — and that should never stand between someone and better hearing. So Dr. Cohen personally brings advanced, portable diagnostic technology to homes and assisted-living communities across greater Los Angeles.

It is the same unhurried, personalized care you would receive in our Sherman Oaks office — delivered in the comfort of your own living room. Simply call 818-989-9001 and we will arrange to come to you.

What we bring to you

  • Full hearing evaluation with portable diagnostic equipment
  • Hearing aid fitting, programming, and verification
  • Cleaning, adjustments, and ongoing follow-up care
  • Unhurried counseling for you and your family

Ideal for

  • Patients with restricted mobility
  • Those without easy transportation
  • Residents of assisted-living communities
  • Anyone who is simply more comfortable at home

Can’t come to us? We’ll come to you.

Call 818-989-9001 to arrange an in-home visit anywhere in the greater Los Angeles area, or send us a message and we will reach out to schedule.
