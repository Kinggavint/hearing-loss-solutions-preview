Areas of Expertise

Hearing care, tinnitus treatment, and hearing protection.

Every service starts with a thorough conversation and a careful evaluation, not a sales pitch.

Interested in: Mobile Visit, Tinnitus Treatment, Musician Hearing Care, Medical-Legal Evaluation. Jump to any section below, or call 818-989-9001 to talk it through with our team.

H

Comprehensive Hearing Evaluation

A full diagnostic audiogram, speech-in-noise testing, and a written summary you can keep and share with your physician.

A

Prescription Hearing Aids

Selection, fitting, and verification with real-ear measurement so the device matches your prescription, not a default setting.

L

Lyric Extended-Wear

Tiny, invisible hearing aids that stay in the ear for months at a time. A strong option for active adults who prefer not to handle their devices daily.

P

Custom Hearing Protection

Musician monitors, motorsport plugs, hunter and shooter protection, and industrial-grade custom earpieces.

B

Auditory Training: Neurotone LACE AI

Neurotone's Listening and Communication Enhancement (LACE AI) software is a computer-based adult auditory training program. It sharpens the brain's ability to process speech in noise and complements hearing aids rather than replacing them, so patients keep improving between visits.

V

Telehealth Follow-Up: Available Statewide

Remote programming adjustments, counseling, and check-ins so you can stay supported without a drive across the Los Angeles area. Telehealth visits are available to established patients anywhere in California.

M

Mobile Hearing Clinic

Dr. Cohen personally travels to your home or assisted-living community for patients who cannot make it to our Sherman Oaks office. The full evaluation, fitting, and follow-up experience, brought to you.

C

Central Auditory Processing (CAPD)

Full CAPD evaluations for children with IEPs, adults on the autism spectrum, and post-injury patients. One of the few practices in Los Angeles offering this specialty.

E

Cochlear Implant Programming

Mapping, programming, and rehabilitation for cochlear implant recipients. Patients travel from across the region for post-implant care.

W

Ear Wax Removal

Safe, professional cerumen management using medical-grade tools. Recommended over at-home cleaning, especially for hearing aid wearers.

M

Musician Hearing Care

Custom in-ear monitors built from your own ear impressions, so stage mixes stay clear without the volume that damages hearing over a career. We fit musician-specific attenuation filters that lower sound levels evenly across frequencies, preserving tone instead of just muffling it. For touring and studio musicians, we also provide hearing conservation counseling and protection built around the instrument you play and the venues you play in.

L

Medical-Legal Evaluation

Dr. Cohen provides expert-witness capacity for hearing-related personal-injury and workers' compensation cases, including independent hearing evaluations, defensible written reports, and deposition testimony. Attorneys and claims professionals rely on clear, clinically sound documentation that holds up under review.

Tinnitus Treatments

A complete tinnitus program, not a single device.

Every tinnitus profile is different. We evaluate pitch, loudness, masking, and daily impact, then match you to the therapy or combination of therapies that fits your case.

L

Lenire

We are a designated Lenire treatment office. Lenire is an FDA-cleared bimodal neuromodulation device that pairs tongue stimulation with sound therapy for chronic subjective tinnitus.

S

Levo

A sleep-based sound therapy program that plays a personalized sound profile while you sleep, helping the brain habituate to tinnitus over a structured course of nightly use.

N

Neuromonics

A structured sound-therapy program that combines acoustic stimulation with a set course of listening sessions, designed to reduce the prominence of tinnitus over time.

M

Music Therapy

Customized, tinnitus-tailored music programs used as a calming, evidence-informed complement to other therapies, especially for patients who respond well to sound-based approaches.

O

Other

Individualized tinnitus retraining therapy (TRT), referrals for cognitive behavioral therapy, and masking devices for patients whose profile calls for a different combination of tools.
