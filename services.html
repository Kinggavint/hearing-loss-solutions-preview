Services

Hearing care, tinnitus treatment, and hearing protection.

Every service starts with a thorough conversation and a careful evaluation, not a sales pitch.

H

Comprehensive Hearing Evaluation

A full diagnostic audiogram, speech-in-noise testing, and a written summary you can keep and share with your physician.

A

Prescription Hearing Aids

Selection, fitting, and verification with real-ear measurement so the device matches your prescription, not a default setting.

L

Lyric Extended-Wear

Tiny, invisible hearing aids that stay in the ear for months at a time. Great for active adults who want zero daily handling.

T

Tinnitus Evaluation

A complete tinnitus assessment that measures pitch, loudness, masking, and impact on daily life, with a personalized treatment plan.

L

Lenire Tinnitus Treatment

We are a designated Lenire treatment office. Lenire is an FDA-cleared bimodal neuromodulation device for chronic subjective tinnitus.

L

Levo and Neuromonics

Additional clinically validated tinnitus therapies for patients who respond better to sound-based or sleep-based programs.

P

Custom Hearing Protection

Musician monitors, motorsport plugs, hunter and shooter protection, and industrial-grade custom earpieces.

B

Auditory Care After Brain Injury

Specialized testing and rehabilitation for patients with concussion or TBI experiencing hearing, tinnitus, or sound tolerance changes.

V

Telehealth Follow-Up

Remote programming adjustments, counseling, and check-ins so you can stay supported without a drive across Los Angeles.

How We Bill

An out-of-network, concierge model.

We are not contracted with insurance plans. That choice protects our time with you. After your visit, we provide an itemized super bill you can submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement under your out-of-network benefits.
