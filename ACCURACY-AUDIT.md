## Needs Dr. Cohen's confirmation — do not publish until answered

### 1. "Out-of-network by design" and super bills — highest risk

The homepage carries a whole section stating:

> *"We are an out-of-network practice… We provide super bills you can submit to
> your insurance for possible reimbursement."*

- **"out-of-network" appears 0 times** across her 139 pages.
- **"super bill" appears 0 times.**
- Her own appointment form asks patients to **"Indicate your insurance."**

This is a claim about money, and the evidence points the other way. If she does
bill insurance, this is actively misleading to patients. It came from the
original AI build, not from her. **Confirm or cut before this goes anywhere
near patients.**

### 2. "Designated Lenire Provider"

- **Lenire appears 0 times on her entire site** — but she confirmed on the
  2026-08-10 call that Lenire ("linear") is *"one of the bigger things that we
  do"* and that it was missing from her site. So the service is real.
- What is unverified is the **specific title**. "Designated Lenire Provider" is
  worded as a formal designation. Peachtree displays a Neuromod-issued
  "Preferred Provider" seal for a specific award tier. Ask which tier, if any,
  she holds, and get the official asset.

### 3. Sunday appointments

Her site supports **Saturday** (277 mentions). **"Weekend" and Sunday appear
nowhere.** Our copy promises "Saturday and Sunday appointments available by
request." Drop Sunday unless she confirms.

### 4. Veterans

Her site says **military** (279) and **first responders** (278). **"Veteran"
appears 0 times.** Our copy says "active-duty military, veterans, and first
responders." Likely fine, but it is an addition we made.

### 5. "Telehealth"

Her site never uses the word — she says **"video consultation"** (276). Not a
false claim, just our vocabulary rather than hers. Worth aligning if she
prefers her own term.
