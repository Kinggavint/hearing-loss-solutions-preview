Patient forms & your first visit

A little preparation makes your first appointment smoother and gives us more time for what matters — you. Here’s what to expect and how to get your new-patient paperwork ahead of time.

What your first visit includes

  • A review of your medical and hearing history
  • An in-depth conversation about your concerns and goals
  • A clear explanation of our process, timeline, and options
  • Recommended evaluation or testing, if indicated
  • A treatment plan tailored to how you live and listen

What to bring

  • A photo ID and your insurance information
  • A list of current medications
  • Any prior hearing test results or audiograms
  • Current hearing aids, if you wear them
  • A family member or friend, if that helps you

Request your new-patient forms.

We’re happy to send your paperwork ahead of time so you can complete it at home, or have it ready for you in the office. Just ask — call 818-989-9001 or email us.
