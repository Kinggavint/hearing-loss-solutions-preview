Why Patients Stay

Time, Expertise, and Follow-Through.

The comments patients leave tend to circle the same three things: that the appointment was unhurried, that the testing was thorough enough to explain what previous providers had not, and that Dr. Cohen kept following up long after the device was fitted.

That is the point of a concierge practice. Appointments are not squeezed to a template, so an evaluation takes as long as it takes and a fitting is verified rather than assumed.

  • 60 to 90 minutes for a first comprehensive evaluation
  • Real-ear measurement to verify every hearing aid fitting
  • A written summary of your results that you keep
  • Care in English, Spanish, and Farsi
