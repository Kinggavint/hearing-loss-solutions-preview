HEARING AND BRAIN HEALTH

Untreated Hearing Loss Raises the Risk of Dementia.

Do not let time and memories slip away.

Research links untreated hearing loss to significantly higher rates of cognitive decline. Treating it is one of the few modifiable risk factors you actually control, and the earlier it is addressed the better the outcome.
