Custom hearing protection

An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure. Sounds above 85 decibels can cause permanent hearing loss — and custom-fit protection lets you stay in the environments you love without paying for it later.

Prevention That Fits

Protection built for your ears and your world.

Off-the-shelf plugs muffle everything and rarely fit well, so people stop wearing them. Custom protection is molded to your ears and tuned to your activity — comfortable enough to actually wear, and precise enough to preserve the sounds you need while blocking the ones that damage hearing.

Roughly 22 million U.S. workers are exposed to hazardous noise on the job. Whether your risk comes from work, a stage, or a firing range, we fit protection that matches it.

Know Your Exposure

How loud is too loud?

NIOSH/CDC permissible exposure times — every 3 dB increase doubles the sound intensity and halves the safe listening time.

Sound Level Safe exposure
City traffic (inside car)85 dB8 hours
Jazz concert91 dB2 hours
Power mower94 dB1 hour

Who We Fit

Protection for every high-noise life.

Musicians

Custom in-ear monitors and musician filters that lower volume evenly across frequencies — preserving tone instead of just muffling it. See musician hearing care ›

Hunters & Shooters

Electronic protection that lets you hear soft sounds and hold a normal conversation while reducing the peak blast of gunfire (often 130+ dB) to a safe level.

Law Enforcement

For officers, a weapon fired in a confined space can cause irreversible loss. We fit protection that guards hearing without compromising situational awareness.

Broadcast & Media

Custom-fit in-ear monitors for newscasters, journalists, and reporters — protecting against dangerous sound levels while feeding you clean audio.

Industrial & Occupational

Properly fitted workplace protection plus periodic testing and worker education — a NIOSH-aligned hearing conservation program. More on occupational injuries ›

Motorsports & More

From the track to the tarmac, we fit protection tuned to the specific sound profile you face — comfortable enough to wear every time.
