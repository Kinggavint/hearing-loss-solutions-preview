Hearing care near Studio City, CA

Just minutes from Studio City, Hearing Loss Solutions offers expert hearing and tinnitus care — including specialized musician hearing care for the neighborhood’s many working artists — plus in-home and telehealth options.

Serving Studio City Since 2001

Trusted by the neighborhood — and its musicians.

Studio City patients have relied on Hearing Loss Solutions since 2001 for careful diagnostics and genuinely personal care. Given the neighborhood’s deep ties to music and media, we are a natural fit: Dr. Cohen fits custom in-ear monitors and musician-grade protection that guard hearing without sacrificing tone.

Beyond the stage and studio, we treat the full range of hearing and tinnitus needs, with rehabilitation, counseling, and emotional support. Can’t make it in? Our Mobile Hearing Clinic comes to you, or we meet by telehealth.
