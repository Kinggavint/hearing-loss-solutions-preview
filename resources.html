Patient Resources

Resources, education, and answers to common questions.

The more our patients understand, the better the outcome. Start with the questions below or browse our learning library.

Plan on 60 to 90 minutes. We begin with a conversation about your history and goals, complete a full diagnostic evaluation, and then walk through findings together. You leave with a written summary and a clear set of options. There is no pressure to make a decision in the chair.
We are out of network with all insurance plans. After your visit we provide a super bill you can submit for possible reimbursement under your plan's out-of-network benefits. We are happy to explain pricing before you book.
There is no single cure for tinnitus, but the majority of patients experience meaningful relief with a structured, evidence-based program. Treatment plans are personalized and may combine sound therapy, devices like Lenire or Levo, counseling, and lifestyle changes.
Lenire is an FDA-cleared bimodal neuromodulation device that pairs tongue stimulation with sound therapy to retrain how the brain processes tinnitus. We are a designated treatment office, which means we are trained and certified to deliver the program correctly.
Yes. Telehealth follow-ups are available for programming adjustments, counseling, and check-ins so you do not need to drive in for every small change.
Our adult-focused practice serves teens and adults across all age groups. For younger children we are happy to refer you to a trusted pediatric audiology colleague.
Modern hearing aids typically last five to seven years before a technology refresh is warranted. We service and maintain devices throughout their full lifecycle.
