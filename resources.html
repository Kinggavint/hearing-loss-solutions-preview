Programs and Financing

Ways to Make Care Work for Your Life.

Concierge care should be accessible. Here are the programs, appreciation discounts, and financing options we offer alongside your treatment plan.

VIP Program

Membership tier with priority scheduling, annual clean and check appointments, warranty tracking, and preferred pricing on accessories. Ask us for full details at your consultation.

45-Day Trial Period

Every hearing aid fitting includes a 45-day acclimation and adjustment period. If the device is not right for your case, we work with you to fine-tune or reselect the technology.

CareCredit Financing

Flexible monthly payment plans, including zero-interest promotional financing for qualifying patients. Apply through CareCredit before or after your visit.

Military and First Responder Appreciation

Reduced pricing on select devices and program packages for active-duty military, veterans, police, fire, and EMS professionals. Bring verification to your consultation.

Same-Day Appointment Starts

When your evaluation and treatment plan can be combined, we work to start same-day. This is common for sudden hearing changes and second opinions.

Mobile Hearing Clinic

Full evaluation, fitting, and follow-up brought to your home or assisted-living community across the greater Los Angeles area.
