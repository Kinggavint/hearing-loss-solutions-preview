Plan on 60 to 90 minutes. We begin with a conversation about your history and goals, complete a full diagnostic evaluation, and then walk through findings together. You leave with a written summary and a clear set of options. There is no pressure to make a decision in the chair.
We are out of network with all insurance plans. After your visit, we provide a super bill you can submit for possible reimbursement under your plan's out-of-network benefits. We are happy to explain pricing before you book.
A super bill is an itemized receipt with the diagnosis and procedure codes your insurer needs to process an out-of-network claim. You pay us directly at the time of service, then submit the super bill to your insurance plan, which may reimburse part of the cost depending on your out-of-network benefits.
Lenire is an FDA-cleared bimodal neuromodulation device that pairs tongue stimulation with sound therapy to retrain how the brain processes tinnitus. We are a designated Lenire treatment office, trained and certified to deliver the program correctly.

There is no single cure for tinnitus, but the majority of patients experience meaningful relief with a structured, evidence-based program. Treatment plans are personalized and may combine sound therapy, devices like Lenire, Levo, or Neuromonics, counseling, and lifestyle changes.
