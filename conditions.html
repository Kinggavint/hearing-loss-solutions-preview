Conditions We Treat

From Mild Hearing Change to Chronic Tinnitus, We Have a Plan.

Audiology covers more than hearing tests. Below is a quick guide to the conditions we evaluate and treat every week.

Age-Related Hearing Loss

Gradual loss of higher frequencies, often noticed as "I can hear, but I cannot understand." Highly treatable with the right device and counseling.

What is hearing loss? ›

Noise-Induced Hearing Loss

From concerts, power tools, firearms, motorsports, and certain occupations. Prevention is the priority; protection is custom-fit.

Sudden Sensorineural Hearing Loss

Sudden sensorineural hearing loss is a medical urgency, with the best outcomes when treated within 72 hours. If your hearing drops suddenly in one ear, call us today. We hold same-day slots for these cases.

Chronic Tinnitus

Ringing, buzzing, or hissing that lasts longer than three months. Treatment is multimodal and tailored to your specific tinnitus profile. See our full range of tinnitus treatments, including Lenire, Levo, Neuromonics, and music therapy.

Hyperacusis

Reduced tolerance for everyday sound. A structured desensitization plan can restore your sound tolerance over months, not years.

Misophonia

Strong emotional reactions to specific sounds. We coordinate with mental health professionals when indicated and provide auditory tools.

Concussion and TBI

Hearing, tinnitus, and sound sensitivity changes are common after a head injury. As a Certified Brain Injury Specialist, Dr. Cohen evaluates and treats these auditory effects.

Single-Sided Deafness

Loss in one ear can be addressed with CROS devices, BiCROS systems, or in some cases bone-conduction technology after medical clearance.

Auditory Processing Difficulty

Trouble understanding in noise even with normal hearing thresholds. Assessment, accommodations, and assistive tools can all help.

What is APD? ›

Hearing, Dementia & Diabetes

Untreated hearing loss is strongly linked to cognitive decline, and is about twice as common in adults with diabetes. Treating it early helps protect brain health.

The hearing-brain connection ›

Dizziness & Balance

Feeling unsteady, woozy, or like the room is spinning? Balance is closely tied to the inner ear, from BPPV to Ménière’s disease.

About balance disorders ›

Occupational Hearing Injuries

Noise above 85 decibels can cause permanent, preventable hearing loss. Custom protection, testing, and workplace hearing conservation.
